Who built this

Built by Hai Le

I day trade Nasdaq futures. I trade off order-flow levels instead of pattern theories, and I built my own AI trading bot. This site is the guide I wish I had when I started: the mechanics of the auction, explained plainly, with tools you can touch.

Education only. Nothing here is financial advice. Futures trading carries substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for everyone. All market data on this site is simulated for teaching.

What went into it

  • A live order-book simulator: market orders consume resting liquidity, price ticks when a level empties.
  • A canvas footprint chart with diagonal imbalance detection, bar POC and a 70% value area.
  • A tutor bot, Tape, that answers order-flow questions in plain language.
  • Seven lessons written from my own trading process on NQ.
